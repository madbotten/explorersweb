# Explorers

These are the files for **Explorers**, a science fiction tactical RPG management game in which you recruit, equip, position, and dispatch a team of explorers on dangerous missions.

## Game Stages

1. **Choose Team** — Recruit 6 humanoid team members from 100 candidates, reviewing their stats, sub-stats, skills, and background history.
2. **Mission Orders** — Review the mission briefing and choose your team's operational methods, means, and options.
3. **Equip Team** — Purchase weapons, armor, gadgets, and up to 4 support robots for your team. Use the mouse wheel or scroll buttons to browse the store catalog.
   - **Weapons & Armor**: Match ranged and close-combat weapons across all damage types (energy, sonic, impact, slash, pierce, concussion, and electric) to each character's combat skills and mission means. Each character may carry up to two weapons and up to two armor pieces: at most one **helmet** and at most one **body armor** (such as a power shield, veer suit, flak vest, insulated suit, chitin carapace, exoskeleton, holofier, or powered armor).
   - **Helmets & Specialized Suits**: Sonic helmets dampen sonic attacks and improve detection; targeting helmets sharpen ranged accuracy, sharpshooting, and perception; and command helmets bolster leadership, squad tactics, military science, and morale resilience under fire. Insulated suits shield against electric weapons and chamber hazard leaks, flak vests absorb concussive blasts and projectiles, chitin carapaces deflect blades and piercing strikes, and powered armor eliminates weight encumbrance. Robots are immune to sonic attacks but take extra damage from electric weapons.
   - **Support Gadgets**: Scanners improve analysis, detection, and stealth reconnaissance; stealth orbs aid combat evasion and stealth checks; stimulants raise maximum health for overhealing; neural trainers grant basic proficiency and accelerate skill learning; bio containers and cutters unlock mission chambers; and pacifiers allow non-lethal capture of high-value targets, traitors, and psychotic crew members.
4. **Position Team** — Assign a **Mission Commander** and **Executive Officer** (non-robots only) and place every team member (including Commander, Executive Officer, and robots) into a movement formation box (**Point Man**, **Center**, **Overwatch**, or **Protected Specialists**). Click any character icon to inspect their stats, skills, and equipment.
   - **Mission Commander & Executive Officer**: Lead the crew during the voyage and direct squad tactics in battle. If the Mission Commander falls, the Executive Officer assumes command.
   - **Point Man**: Scouts ahead to help the team avoid detection and strike quickly when combat begins, though they draw heavy early attention from enemies. Scout robots are especially effective in this role, while leaving the position empty leaves the squad vulnerable to being caught off guard.
   - **Center**: Forms the main battle line, engaging enemies in close quarters, shielding the rear positions, and supporting one another when fighting side by side.
   - **Overwatch**: Provides ranged covering fire from behind the front line while avoiding close-quarters combat as long as the Center holds.
   - **Protected Specialists**: Kept shielded behind the front and support lines so they avoid the brunt of enemy attacks and stay fresh to handle non-combat challenges.
5. **Voyage** — Watch your team train, interact, and handle shipboard events on the outbound journey.
6. **Mission** — Follow your team through rooms, challenges, and tactical battles to achieve the mission objective.
7. **Return Voyage** — Navigate the journey back to base while guarding against conspiracies and hazards.
8. **Score** — Review mission points earned, convert surviving robots back into credits, and proceed to the next mission loop.
